Automation projects

Automation project

Monthly account book builder

The challenge

Building a monthly master list needs payment history pulled from six separate monthly files.

The approach

Merges six months of payment files, totals payments per account, counts the months each account paid, groups accounts into payment-frequency buckets and adds balances and data-quality flags.

The output

One clean monthly master file, ready for the next stage.

Automation project

Eligible account filtering

The challenge

Before work is assigned, accounts must be filtered by clear, consistent rules.

The approach

Removes flagged accounts, keeps only valid records above a minimum balance, excludes accounts on a special list and tags who handled each account last month.

The output

A lean file with only the required columns, ready for assignment.

Automation project

Team allocation mapping

The challenge

Lists from many teams must be combined into one view of who owns each account.

The approach

Matches every team's list to the master book, builds a single owner column and cleans up overlapping labels.

The output

A master book showing the final owner of every account.

Automation project

Payment behaviour analysis

The challenge

Leadership wants to see how payment behaviour differs by team and by delay stage.

The approach

Adds the month's payments and contact status to the book, groups accounts by payment frequency and compares totals and counts for contacted and non-contacted accounts.

The output

A summary table by team and delay stage, ready for review.

Automation project

Historical trend report

The challenge

Seeing month-over-month movement means combining many monthly files by hand.

The approach

Loads several months of payment files, removes payment types that should not count, totals payments per account and groups them by team.

The output

An Excel trend report with monthly totals for each team.

Automation project

Customer pipeline report

The challenge

Tracking prospects through each stage, and checking who is already onboarded, is slow across many files.

The approach

Combines credit bureau and disbursement files, finds customers not yet onboarded, flags positive credit status and shows how far each one has moved through the pipeline stages.

The output

A three-sheet Excel workbook for the pipeline team.

Automation project

Daily partner reports

The challenge

Daily files from several partner teams must be combined, refreshed and sent back separately.

The approach

Loads each team's file, standardises the columns, refreshes balances from the latest report, adjusts for recoveries and splits the result by team.

The output

Ready-to-share daily files, one per team.

Automation project

Daily business snapshot

The challenge

Leadership needs the same set of business numbers every morning.

The approach

Compares today with yesterday and last month to find additions, reductions and net change, totals payments by team group and adds new disbursement figures.

The output

The day's key numbers, calculated the same way every time.

Automation project

Branch-wise summary

The challenge

Comparing opening and closing positions by branch takes hours when done manually.

The approach

Loads the opening and closing portfolios, assigns delay stages, brings in demand, collection and headcount files and groups everything by branch.

The output

A branch-level summary ready for review.
